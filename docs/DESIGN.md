# Design notes

Peeksh is a small native utility with a capable terminal inside. Keep everyday actions close and deeper controls in Settings.

- Use restrained color, readable monospaced text, and short transitions. Respect Reduce Motion and Reduce Transparency.
- Keep terminal output on a stable, solid surface. Reserve glass for controls, where it communicates hierarchy without competing with text.
- Use one resizable terminal window opened from the menu-bar icon. Remember its size and per-display position; hiding it must never destroy a session.
- Keep presentation controls quiet and advanced preferences in Settings. The terminal content takes priority; the global toggle shortcut is optional.
- Use familiar terminal shortcuts, independently running split panes, and native draggable dividers.
- Keep developer information at the bottom of Settings and in a separate About window.

References: [Apple materials](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/materials), [Adopting Liquid Glass](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/TechnologyOverviews/adopting-liquid-glass), [Ghostty features](https://ghostty.org/docs/features), [iTerm2 highlights](https://iterm2.com/documentation-highlights.html).
