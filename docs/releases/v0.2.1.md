Two terminal modes, with a quieter window frame.

- The split-panel button offers Split Right, Split Down, and Focus Pane. Floating Window and Top Drawer are available at the top of More options (⋯), in View, and in the menu bar icon's right-click menu.
- Window bars have a stronger background tint while retaining their blurred material.
- Tabs and split panes use clear Home and Root labels, with full paths available on hover.
- The README pairs real terminal captures with a designed desktop background and presents both window modes.

Open **term-cut.dmg** and drag **term-cut** into **Applications**. Supports Apple silicon and Intel on macOS 13 or later.

This build is ad-hoc signed without Apple notarization; first launch may require approval in System Settings → Privacy & Security.
