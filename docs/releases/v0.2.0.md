A smaller toolbar. A calmer terminal.

- Tabs and controls now share one thin, translucent row. The extra title header and control capsule are gone.
- Kanagawa Dragon is the default theme, with its terminal colors, selection background, and ANSI palette.
- A custom prompt-and-cut menu bar icon makes term-cut easier to recognize.
- Removed the persistent scroll indicator. Scrollback still works with the mouse wheel and trackpad.
- Tabs remember their selected pane, keep the active tab visible when resizing, and preserve pane zoom when another tab closes.
- Settings search finds full labels and theme names. Restore Defaults resets the window mode, and older or partially damaged preferences retain their valid values.
- Closing panes, tabs, or the app asks before stopping running commands or background jobs. Cancelling keeps the sessions open.

Download **term-cut.dmg**, open it, and drag **term-cut** onto **Applications**. The release provides a universal application for Apple silicon and Intel; no application ZIP is needed.

Requires macOS 13 or later. This build is ad-hoc signed without Apple notarization; first launch may require approval in System Settings → Privacy & Security.
