Your terminal. One shortcut away.

The first public release of term-cut, a native menu bar terminal for macOS.

- Compact panel and drop-down terminal sharing one workspace.
- Tabs, horizontal and vertical splits, pane focus, and terminal search.
- Persistent split proportions while switching tabs or focusing panes.
- Three terminal themes, installed monospaced fonts, custom cursors, and configurable window sizes.
- Custom global shortcuts, launch at login, and accessibility-aware transitions.

Download **term-cut.dmg**, open it, and drag **term-cut** into **Applications**.

Requires macOS 13 or later. The application is universal: Apple silicon and Intel.

This build is ad-hoc signed, without an Apple Developer ID or notarization. macOS may require approval in **System Settings → Privacy & Security** on first launch. Building from source is also supported.

Sessions continue while the window is hidden and end when you close them or quit term-cut. They are not restored after quitting. Working-directory inheritance requires OSC 7 support from your shell.
